# Portuguese dictionary data (dict/pt/)

The files in this folder are derived from the **English Wiktionary** (Portuguese entries), extracted with
[wiktextract](https://github.com/tatuylonen/wiktextract) and published by [kaikki.org](https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Portuguese/).
They were shortened and reformatted by `tools/build_ptdict.py`.
